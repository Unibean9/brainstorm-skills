04 / insightTrust needs a place

A shared rule works better when it has a shared place.

Physical cabinet + QR record + one steward = a visible custody loop.

The differentiator is not more messaging. It is a concrete handoff: where the item is, who holds it, and what happens next.

Community CabinetInsight / trust made operational
Speaker note: This is the solution thesis. It connects the social awkwardness and the lender's risk without introducing a new marketplace.

11 / impactWhat to learn

Success is a return loop that earns another lend.

Primary signal

Lenders come back.

Repeat lending is a directional signal; numeric target remains open.

Primary signal

Items return on time.

Define the expected-return window before measuring.

Counter-signal

Do not optimize volume.

More items or more borrows cannot justify weaker custody or damage handling.

Community CabinetImpact / signals, not claims
Speaker note: No numeric traction is claimed. The committee should decide the definitions and targets before the pilot review.
12 / risksWhat can break trust

Keep the failure modes visible too.

DAMAGE / LOSS

Mitigate with custody history, inspection, and an approved rule.

AVAILABILITY DRIFT

Mitigate with one Steward and physical/record reconciliation.

STEWARD OVERLOAD

Keep the pilot to one neighborhood; define backup coverage.

ACCESS INTERRUPTION

Define an outage and non-scan fallback before launch.

Community CabinetRisks / mitigations + open decisions
