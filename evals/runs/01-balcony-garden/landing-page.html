The ten-second question

A plant looks sick. The answer isn’t obvious.

People can buy a plant excitedly, then a few weeks later see it look sick and not know what they did wrong. For someone working during the day, the repeated question is smaller and more urgent than learning a whole garden:

“Does this plant need water today?”

The mechanism

Put the guidance where care happens.

The Care Card sits in the pot, ready for the moment the plant is already in view.
