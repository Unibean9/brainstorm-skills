- idea (by user): student groups need a fairer way to brainstorm in class.
- problem (by user): one fast, confident student can pull the group toward their own idea.
- problem (by user): quieter students may have good ideas but cannot enter the conversation.
- constraint (by user): a teacher cannot stand in the middle of every group and manage every speaking turn.
- idea (by user): candidate mechanisms include speaking tokens, writing before talking, and a bot that reminds people to make room.
- idea (by user): a voice facilitator could ask one short question at a time and record what each person contributed.
- idea (by user): the facilitator could bring back ideas mentioned once and then forgotten.
- insight (by user): a shy student needs a speaking experience that feels safe.
- insight (by user): a teacher needs visibility into whether AI is steering the answer.
- constraint (by user): a fifteen-minute group needs short prompts, not a lecture.
- risk (by user): AI praise could make one idea seem like the correct answer.
- risk (by user): recording who said what could make students afraid of being judged.
- risk (by user): a saved transcript could be used for grading without group agreement.
- risk (by user): voice transcription could misunderstand a student and create a false trace.
- decision (by user): default mode asks questions only; a bounded-suggestion mode requires explicit teacher enablement.
