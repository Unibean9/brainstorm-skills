01 / the problem
Dominance.

One fast, confident student can pull the group toward their own idea.

Quieter students

May have good ideas but cannot enter the conversation.

Teacher bandwidth

Cannot stand in the middle of every group and manage every speaking turn.

Classroom clock

A group with fifteen minutes needs short prompts, not a lecture.

Tablemate · problem framing
02 / 12
The problem is not that students lack ideas. It is that the conversational surface can reward speed and confidence, while the teacher cannot manually rebalance every group.
02 / audience

Two perspectives.
One shared room.

Student group

Make room to contribute.

A group that needs a short prompt, a place for ideas, and a way to revisit what was almost forgotten.

Teacher

Stay in control.

An overseer who needs to know whether AI is steering the answer — without standing inside every group.

Decision audience

See the boundary.

School decision-makers need the v1 promise stated narrowly; deployment and governance questions remain open.

06 / product experience

A trace that remembers
without pretending.

Contribution

A student idea enters the session.

Uncertainty

Voice transcription may be a False Trace.

Recall

An idea mentioned once returns to the room.

Development

AI Provenance stays attached if the idea evolves.

Not a verdict

No idea is praised into becoming “the correct answer.”

Not a score

The trace is not a grading record in v1.

Not a certainty

Machine transcription does not outrank the student’s meaning.

Tablemate · MVP boundary
10 / 12
Scope is intentionally narrow because the trust boundaries are load-bearing. The open items are not polish: privacy, provenance, and grading-use decisions must be resolved before saved traces are treated as a release-ready workflow.
10 / risks

The hard questions are
part of the product.

Could recording reduce participation?

Attribution may make students afraid of judgment. Identity visibility and consent are open.

Could a trace become a grade?

A saved Transcript may be used for grading without agreement. Policy is unresolved.

Could the machine be wrong?

Voice transcription can create a False Trace. Uncertainty and correction behavior are required.

Could AI quietly lead?

Praise can make one idea appear correct. Question-only is the default.

Could the tool become a lecture?

Fifteen minutes leaves no room for long facilitation. Short-question behavior needs review.

Could metrics create false confidence?

No numeric target is confirmed. Baseline and method stay open.

Tablemate · risk register
